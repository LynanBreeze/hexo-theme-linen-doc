## Overview

This sample article dated 2025 demonstrates several Linen features together:

- **Multi-year grouping (`2025` / `2026`)** on the Archives page
- **Article subtitle** (`subTitle: 2025`) rendered next to the title
- **Post age notice banner** (`ageWarning: true`), automatically displayed once a post is older than 180 days
- **Related posts** at the bottom when sharing 2 or more tags with another article

## Typography

This paragraph illustrates standard body text, inline code, and blockquotes. Linen uses restrained font sizing, generous line height, and balanced margins so readers stay focused on the writing itself.

> Good typography stays out of the way and lets reading feel effortless.
