<h2 id="to-page-top" style="opacity: 0; height: 0; margin: 0;">Introduction</h2>

Took two days off around the Qingming holiday for a cherry blossom trip to Japan's Kanto region. Two main wishes for the trip:

1. Full-bloom cherry blossoms ✅
2. Snow-capped Mt. Fuji ✅

## Route

- Tokyo 2.5 days
- Mt. Fuji 1 day (Lake Kawaguchi, Lake Yamanaka)
- Kamakura 0.5 days

## Itinerary

| Date | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 04/02 | **Shanghai** -> **Tokyo** Kanda River, Shinjuku Gyoen, Lake Kawaguchi |
| Day 2 04/03 | **Mt. Fuji** Lake Kawaguchi, Lake Yamanaka |
| Day 3 04/04 | **Kamakura & Tokyo** Kencho-ji, Chidorigafuchi night sakura |
| Day 4 04/05 | **Tokyo** Ochanomizu, Senso-ji, return flight |

## Schedule

### Day 1 Tokyo

Landed at Haneda early in the morning and took the train straight to Kanda River. Walking beneath the blooming sakura in a light drizzle felt like a scene from a film.

![](/img/travel/kanto-kanda.jpg)

### Day 2 Mt. Fuji

Arrived at Kawaguchiko Bridge under clear, calm morning skies—catching a crisp view of snow-capped Mt. Fuji reflected across the lake.

![](/img/travel/kanto-fuji.jpg)

## Reflections

A compact yet deeply rewarding sakura trip—combining city blossoms with crystal-clear views of Mt. Fuji.
