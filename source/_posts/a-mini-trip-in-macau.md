<h2 id="to-page-top" style="opacity: 0; height: 0; margin: 0;">Introduction</h2>

Grabbed an affordable weekend flight for a quick mini-trip to Macau, exploring its historic streets and unique blend of Chinese and Portuguese culture.

## Route

- Macau Peninsula 1 day (Guia Fortress, Ruins of St. Paul's, Senado Square)
- Cotai & Taipa 1 day (Rua do Cunha, Wetland Park)

## Saturday

Landed at Macau International Airport in the morning, headed into Macau Peninsula, climbed Guia Hill for views over the lighthouse and old town, and strolled to the Ruins of St. Paul's and Senado Square by dusk.

![Guia Lighthouse](/img/travel/macau-lighthouse.jpg)

## Sunday

Visited Rua do Cunha for local snacks and Portuguese egg tarts, took a quiet walk through the wetland park, and caught the evening flight home.

## Reflections

Compact yet full of contrast—two days was just right to experience both the lively historic peninsula and the glittering lights of Cotai.
