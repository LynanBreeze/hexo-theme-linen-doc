Three years after my first visit, I returned to Jiuzhaigou under clear summer skies, followed by a short stop in Chengdu to see giant pandas.

## Route

- Jiuzhaigou 1 day
- Chengdu 1.5 days (Panda Base, Wenshu Monastery, Huanhuaxi)
- Mianyang 0.5 days (Shengshui Temple, Luohan Temple)

## Itinerary

| Date | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 06/12 | **Shanghai** -> **Mianyang** -> **Jiuzhaigou** |
| Day 2 06/13 | **Jiuzhaigou** Full-day valley tour |
| Day 3 06/14 | **Jiuzhaigou** -> **Chengdu** Giant Panda Base |
| Day 4 06/15 | **Chengdu** Wenshu Monastery, Huanhuaxi -> **Mianyang** -> Return |

## Day 1 Heading to Jiuzhaigou

Flew from Shanghai to Mianyang Nanjiao Airport, then took a shuttle north and checked in near the Jiuzhaigou valley entrance in the afternoon.

## Day 2 Jiuzhaigou

Rode the park shuttle through Primeval Forest, Arrow Bamboo Lake, Panda Lake, and Five Flower Lake. Under bright sunlight, the turquoise water looked crystal clear and layered with vibrant colors.

![](/img/travel/sichuan-wuhuahai.jpg)

## Reflections

Summer greenery and clear skies made Jiuzhaigou unforgettable, and strolling through Chengdu's parks and temples made for an unhurried finish.
